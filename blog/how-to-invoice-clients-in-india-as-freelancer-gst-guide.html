How to Invoice Clients in India as a Freelancer — GST Guide With Invoice Template

What to put on your invoice, when GST applies, how to bill foreign clients and how to get paid on time.

✍️ Deep Darji 📅 Published Oct 24, 2026 🔄 Updated Oct 24, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read 🏷 Freelance • GST • Invoicing

Knowing how to invoice clients in India as a freelancer protects your income and keeps you compliant. A professional invoice must show who is billing whom, for what, the amount and any tax. This guide covers GST basics, export invoices and a simple template.

GST rules are detailed and change from time to time, so treat this as a practical overview and confirm specifics with a Chartered Accountant.

Quick answer: Include your name and address, client details, a unique invoice number, date, service description with SAC code, amount, GST (if registered) and payment details. For foreign clients, you can usually invoice as an export of services under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) without charging IGST.

Do You Need GST Registration?

Registration is generally required once your turnover crosses the threshold set by law, which for service providers has been Rs 20 lakh a year in most states (lower in some special-category states). Some inter-state service providers may have relaxations, and registration is needed in certain other situations. Check the official GST portal and speak to a CA for your case.

If you are not registered, you issue a simple invoice or a "bill of supply" without charging GST.

What Every GST Invoice Must Include

  • Your name, address and GSTIN.
  • A unique, consecutive invoice number and the date.
  • Client's name, address and GSTIN (if they have one).
  • Description of service with the SAC code (for example, 998314 for IT design and development services; verify the right code for your work).
  • Taxable value, tax rate and tax amount.
  • Place of supply and your signature (digital is fine).

CGST, SGST or IGST?

SituationTax charged
Client in the same stateCGST + SGST
Client in a different stateIGST
Client outside India (export of services)Zero-rated; usually under LUT with no IGST

Many professional and IT services attract GST at 18%, but always confirm the current rate for your service.

Invoicing Foreign Clients

  1. File an LUT on the GST portal each financial year (if registered).
  2. Mention "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST" on the invoice.
  3. Show the amount in the foreign currency and, if needed, the INR equivalent.
  4. Receive payment in convertible foreign exchange and keep your bank advice or e-FIRC as proof.

TDS and Getting Paid

Indian companies may deduct TDS on professional fees before paying you. Track it in your Form 26AS or AIS and claim credit when filing your income tax return. Ask clients early whether they will deduct TDS so your cash flow is not surprised.

  • State payment terms on every invoice (for example, net 7 or net 15).
  • Send a polite reminder one day before and one day after the due date.
  • Take an advance on larger projects.

Common Invoicing Mistakes

  • Skipping invoice numbering or reusing numbers.
  • Charging GST when not registered.
  • Wrong state or place of supply, leading to the wrong tax type.
  • No written payment terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do freelancers in India need to charge GST?

Only when registered under GST, which is generally required above the turnover threshold or in specific cases. Confirm with a Chartered Accountant.

How do I invoice a foreign client from India?

Issue an export invoice, usually under an LUT without IGST, and receive payment in convertible foreign exchange. Keep bank proof for records.

What is a SAC code?

A Service Accounting Code identifies the type of service on a GST invoice.

What is TDS on freelancer payments?

It is tax deducted at source by a client when paying professional fees. You can claim credit for it in your tax return.
