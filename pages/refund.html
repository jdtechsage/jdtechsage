Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how payments, cancellations, and refunds are handled for services provided by JD TECH SAGE.

Overview

General Policy

At JD TECH SAGE, we provide customized IT services including app development, game development, web solutions, networking infrastructure, and CCTV systems. Due to the nature of these services, refunds are handled under specific conditions outlined below.

Eligibility

When Refunds Apply

⏳

Before Project Start

Full or partial refund may be issued if the project has not yet started.

⚠️

Service Failure

If we fail to deliver agreed services due to internal issues, partial refunds may be considered.

📄

Mutual Agreement

Refunds may be issued if both parties agree to cancel the project under fair conditions.

Non-Refundable

Non-Refundable Cases

🚀

Work Started

No refunds once project work has begun or resources have been allocated.

🎨

Custom Development

Custom-built apps, websites, or systems are non-refundable once delivered.

🔄

Change of Mind

No refunds for client decision changes after agreement or approval stages.

📦

Third-Party Costs

Payments made for domains, hosting, APIs, licenses, or hardware are non-refundable.

📷

Hardware Installations

CCTV and networking hardware once installed are not eligible for refunds.

💻

Delivered Milestones

Completed and approved milestones are non-refundable.

Cancellation

Project Cancellation

  • Clients may cancel before work begins for eligible refunds.
  • Once development starts, cancellation may incur charges based on work completed.
  • JD TECH SAGE reserves the right to cancel projects due to policy violations or non-payment.
Process

Refund Process

📩

Request

Submit a refund request via email or official contact channels.

🔍

Review

We evaluate the request based on project status and agreement terms.

💰

Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days.

Payments

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued using the original payment method unless otherwise agreed. Any transaction fees or charges imposed by payment gateways may be deducted.

Updates

Policy Updates

JD TECH SAGE may update this Refund Policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page with revised details.

Questions About Refunds?

Contact us before starting your project for complete clarity on payments and policies.
