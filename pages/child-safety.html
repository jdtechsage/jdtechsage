Child Safety & CSAE Policy

JD Tech Sage maintains an absolute, unconditional zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in every product, platform, and service we build and operate.

📅 Effective Date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: July 2026
🚫 Zero Tolerance — No Exceptions
🚨

If you have seen child abuse content — report it immediately.

Do not wait. Email us at safecircle.jdtechsage@gmail.com or contact the NCMEC CyberTipline directly. In India, report to cybercrime.gov.in .

1. Our Statement Against CSAE

JD Tech Sage ("we", "our", "the Company") unequivocally prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any and all forms, across every product, application, website, game, or service we develop, operate, or maintain.

This prohibition is absolute. There are no exceptions. No context, purpose, artistic claim, fictional framing, or technical justification will ever make such content or behaviour acceptable on any platform associated with JD Tech Sage.

We define CSAE to include, but not be limited to:

  • Any visual, audio, written, or AI-generated content that sexually depicts, exploits, or abuses a minor (any person under 18 years of age)
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any format — photos, videos, illustrations, animations, or synthetic/AI-generated depictions
  • Grooming behaviour — communicating with a minor to build trust for the purpose of sexual exploitation or abuse
  • Solicitation of minors for sexual purposes through any channel
  • Trafficking of minors or facilitating access to minors for exploitative purposes
  • Sharing, distributing, linking to, or promoting CSAM or CSAE-related content
  • Using our platforms to arrange, coordinate, or facilitate child abuse

This policy applies to all users, clients, employees, contractors, and any party that interacts with our services.

🚫

2. Prohibited User Behaviour

Users of any JD Tech Sage product, application, or service are strictly prohibited from:

  • Uploading, posting, or sharing any content that constitutes CSAM or CSAE, including real, illustrated, animated, or AI-generated depictions of minors in sexual situations
  • Distributing or linking to CSAE content through our platforms, including via direct messages, public posts, profile content, or embedded media
  • Promoting, glorifying, or normalising the sexual exploitation or abuse of children in any form
  • Grooming minors through our platforms — this includes building inappropriate relationships with minors, requesting personal information from minors, or communicating with minors in a sexualised manner
  • Soliciting CSAM from other users or attempting to purchase, trade, or exchange CSAE material
  • Using our platforms to facilitate, arrange, or coordinate any form of child abuse or exploitation — online or offline
  • Misrepresenting age — creating accounts or content that falsely represents a minor as an adult, or vice versa, for exploitative purposes
  • Circumventing safety measures — attempting to bypass age verification, content filters, or any other child-safety system we implement

Note on AI-generated content: Synthetic, AI-generated, or digitally altered depictions of minors in sexual contexts are explicitly prohibited under this policy and are illegal under applicable law in India and internationally. The fact that no real child was photographed does not make such content acceptable — it remains CSAM under law.

⚖️

3. Enforcement — Account Suspension & Termination

Any user, account, or entity found to be in violation of this policy will face immediate and severe consequences:

  • Immediate account suspension — upon detection or credible report of CSAE-related activity, the account will be suspended pending investigation, without prior warning
  • Permanent account termination — accounts confirmed to have uploaded, shared, or facilitated CSAE content will be permanently banned with no possibility of reinstatement
  • Content removal — all prohibited content will be removed immediately upon discovery
  • Preservation of evidence — we will preserve relevant data, logs, and account information as required by law enforcement and applicable legal obligations
  • Reporting to authorities — we will report confirmed or credible cases to relevant law enforcement agencies and child protection organisations without delay (see Section 5)
  • Legal action — we reserve the right to pursue civil legal action against individuals who use our platforms for CSAE activities

Violations of this policy are not subject to appeal in cases where CSAM has been confirmed. We do not offer second chances for the most serious violations involving child sexual abuse material.

5. Cooperation with Law Enforcement

JD Tech Sage cooperates fully and proactively with law enforcement agencies, child protection authorities, and relevant regulatory bodies in all matters related to child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Our commitments to law enforcement include:

  • Mandatory reporting: We will report confirmed CSAM discoveries to appropriate authorities, including India's Cyber Crime Cell, without requiring a court order. This is both a legal obligation and a moral one.
  • Data preservation: Upon receiving a lawful request or upon discovering credible CSAE activity, we will preserve account data, logs, IP addresses, device information, and any other relevant evidence to assist investigations.
  • Responding to legal process: We will respond promptly and completely to lawful subpoenas, court orders, search warrants, and emergency requests from law enforcement related to child safety matters.
  • Proactive cooperation: We will cooperate with any investigation involving our platforms, including providing technical assistance within our capabilities, without requiring the investigation to proceed to formal legal demands where child safety is at risk.
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: We will make CyberTipline reports to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) for CSAM discovered on our platforms, as required under applicable law.

We understand that protecting children takes priority over user privacy in cases involving child sexual abuse and exploitation. No privacy consideration will prevent us from cooperating with lawful law enforcement requests in CSAE matters.

🛡️

6. Our Internal Child Safety Safeguards

JD Tech Sage takes active steps to prevent CSAE on every platform we build and operate. Our technical and operational safeguards include:

  • Age-appropriate design: Products intended for or likely to attract users under 18 are designed with enhanced safety features, restricted content, and stricter privacy protections by default
  • Content moderation: User-generated content on our platforms is subject to moderation policies that prohibit CSAE material
  • Reporting mechanisms: Every product that allows user interaction includes an accessible mechanism to report safety concerns
  • Staff training: Our team members are trained to recognise and respond to potential CSAE concerns appropriately and without delay
  • Third-party vetting: We vet third-party APIs, SDKs, and services integrated into our products for compliance with child safety standards
  • Privacy for minors: We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we discover we have collected such data inadvertently, we delete it promptly
