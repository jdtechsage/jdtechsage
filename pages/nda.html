Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

At JDTECHSAGE, we take confidentiality seriously. This NDA ensures that your ideas, data, and business information remain fully protected when working with us.

Legal Protection

Our Commitment to Confidentiality

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between JDTECHSAGE ("Company") and the client, partner, or individual ("Disclosing Party"). The purpose of this Agreement is to protect all confidential and proprietary information shared during business engagements including app development, game development, web solutions, networking setup, and CCTV installations.

Definition

📱

Source code, UI/UX designs, app logic, architecture, APIs, and product strategies.

🎮

🌐

Website & Business Info

Business plans, customer data, credentials, hosting details, and admin access.

🔗

Infrastructure design, IP configurations, firewall settings, and enterprise network layouts.

📷

Contracts, pricing, proposals, internal documentation, and confidential discussions.

Responsibilities

Our Obligations

🔒

We do not disclose any client information to third parties without written consent.

⚙️

Limited Usage

Information is used strictly for project execution and service delivery purposes only.

👨‍💻

Secure Access

Only authorized team members have access to confidential project data.

Limitations

Exceptions to Confidentiality

  • Information already publicly available.
  • Data received legally from third parties.
  • Information independently developed without access to confidential data.
  • Disclosure required by law or government authority.
Validity

Agreement Duration

This Agreement remains in effect during the course of the project and continues for a period of 3 years after project completion, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
