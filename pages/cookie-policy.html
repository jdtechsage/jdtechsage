Overview

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide insights into how users interact with content. JDTECHSAGE uses cookies across its website and services related to app development, game development, web solutions, networking, and CCTV systems.
