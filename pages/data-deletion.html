Data Deletion Policy

JDTECHSAGE respects your right to control your data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your personal or project-related information.

Overview

Your Right to Data Deletion

At JDTECHSAGE, we provide services including app development, game development, web solutions, networking, and CCTV systems. You have the right to request deletion of your personal data or project data that we store, subject to legal and contractual limitations.

Scope

Data You Can Request to Delete

👤

Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, and contact details.

📱

App/User Data

User accounts, stored app data, preferences, and activity records.

🌐

Website Data

Form submissions, login accounts, and stored user interactions.

📂

Project Files

Uploaded assets, documents, and project-related materials.

📷

Security Data

CCTV-related stored configurations or access data (where applicable).

📊

Analytics Data

Tracking and usage data where identifiable to the user.

Process

How to Request Data Deletion

📩

Submit Request

Contact us via email or contact form with your deletion request.

🔍

Verification

We may verify your identity to ensure secure processing of your request.

🗑️

Deletion

Approved requests are processed and data is deleted within a reasonable timeframe.

Timeline

Processing Time

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 7–30 business days, depending on the complexity and type of data involved.

Limitations

When Deletion May Not Apply

  • Data required for legal, regulatory, or tax compliance.
  • Information necessary for ongoing contracts or dispute resolution.
  • Backup archives retained for security and recovery purposes.
  • Data anonymized and no longer linked to identifiable individuals.
Third-Party

Third-Party Data

If your data has been shared with third-party services (such as hosting, analytics, or APIs), you may need to contact those providers directly for deletion requests, subject to their policies.

Contact

Request Submission

To request data deletion, please contact us through our official contact page or email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include sufficient details to identify your data.

Updates

Policy Updates

JDTECHSAGE may update this Data Deletion Policy periodically. Updates will be reflected on this page.
