Technical Questions
Flutter vs React Native - which do you recommend?
+

For most projects, we recommend Flutter - it's faster to develop, has a more consistent UI across platforms, and the performance is excellent. React Native is better when you have an existing JavaScript/React team or need deep integration with native modules. We'll recommend based on your specific case. See our comparison blog for game engines too.

Unity vs Unreal Engine - which is better for my game?
+

Unity is better for mobile games, 2D games, and projects with moderate budgets - it has a massive asset store and is faster to prototype with. Unreal is better for AAA-quality 3D visuals on PC/console with larger budgets. For most indie and mid-scale games, we use and recommend Unity. Read our full comparison: Unity vs Unreal Engine →

Yes - 100%. Once final payment is received, all source code, design files, and assets are transferred to you completely. You own everything. We don't hold your code hostage or charge licensing fees. This is clearly stated in our contract.

Can you publish my app to Play Store and App Store?
