How it works

Report. Verify. Protect.

Every SafeCircle report follows the same accountable path from submission to public safety alert.

User creates report

A verified user submits category, description, date, location and optional evidence.

Admin reviews

The report enters a pending queue and is checked by a SafeCircle administrator.

Approve or reject

Admins approve valid reports, or reject with a reason like duplicate or insufficient detail.

Report becomes public

Approved reports are published with reporter information fully removed.

Community is notified

Users in the matching area receive a personalized safety notification.

Inside the app

Everything you need to report and stay informed

The SafeCircle user app covers the full loop — from creating a profile to acting on a verified alert.

Create a profile

Sign up with email or phone and set your country, state, district and city.

Submit reports

Report harassment, assault, crime, fraud, scams and more, with optional evidence images.

Search & filter

Find reports by location, category or keyword, filtered down to your exact city.

Save reports

Bookmark important reports to your profile and revisit them anytime.

Share safely

Every report has a shareable link — safecircle.app/report/id — that opens straight into the app.

Comment & discuss

Add updates or context on approved reports, with moderation and spam protection.

Personalized alerts

Get notified when a new verified report is approved in your own area.

Report detail view

See category, location, date, description and verification status at a glance.

Minimal permissions

SafeCircle only asks for notification, internet, and image access when you upload evidence.

Privacy first

Your identity stays out of public view

  • 01

    Verification without exposure

    Reporters provide name, contact number and email so admins can verify accountability — this is never shown to other users.

  • 02

    Only approved content goes public

    Published reports show category, location, date and description. No reporter details are attached.

  • 03

    Rejected reports stay hidden

    If a report doesn't meet review standards, it's never published, with a clear rejection reason logged.

  • 04

    Role-based admin access

    Only authenticated administrators can review, approve, reject or moderate reports and comments.
