Contact support

Email safecircle.jdtechsage@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. For account or data deletion requests, see our delete account & data page.

Common topics

  • Report not showing publicly? Reports go through admin review before they're published — check the status on your report's detail page in the app.
  • Report was rejected? Rejection reasons include invalid information, insufficient details, duplicate report, or policy violation. You're welcome to resubmit with more detail.
  • Can't sign in? Double-check the email or phone number linked to your account, or reach out to support to reset access.
  • Want to remove your account? See the account & data deletion instructions.
  • Concerned about privacy? Read our full Privacy Policy.
